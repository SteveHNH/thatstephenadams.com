It really is a magical show.

The part of my life that's not quite ready for primetime is work and what not. I'm doing it, of course, but I gotta get **into** it. You know what I mean? It's amazing how a few days off can really throw you out of a routine. I think that happens to everybody though. I've been having to work remotely for a bit since we have a bunch of house stuff going on as well and that hasn't helped. I do a lot better when I'm at the office surrounded by cool people doing cool things. Hoping I can get back in there on Wednesday. That would certainly be good for me.

I also need to come up with some way to make some extra cash. Anybody got any ideas? I feel like there are more tools at my disposal than ever and I'm still trying to figure out how to make it all work. I have this 3D printer over here doing a whole lot of nothing, but I think I could turn it into something if I tried. I guess doing nothing is ... doing nothing. Maybe I should just throw some things out there.

Writing this post has been a good morning workout for my brain. Maybe I'll start doing these more often. Maybe every Monday? We'll see. Until next time!
